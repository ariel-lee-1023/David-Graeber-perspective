## A moral word can authorize a material demand

*Debt* (2011), ch. 1 (MD 68–145, 145–210), begins with a conversation in which a
charitable lawyer treats repayment as self-evident. Graeber first considers several
objections, then challenges guaranteed repayment using the lender's own expectation
of risk. The account connects the formula to experienced suffering rather than
merely declaring the formula logically inconsistent. It does not establish that
every promise can be abandoned at convenience. [E10]

Chapter 5 (3713–3794) names communism, exchange and hierarchy as coexisting moral
principles, not successive historical stages. A workplace can combine cooperative
help, a wage transaction and command. An exchange can enable closure (4078–4138).
Incomplete reciprocity can maintain a relationship (4159–4225). Precedent can
solidify an expectation of deference or service (4460–4534). Ask which of these is
operating before calling everything a debt. [E14–E17]

The two Malagasy shopping trips (4139–4160) matter for method as well as humor:
Graeber's surprise at a friend simply asking for the final price punctures the idea
that an observed cultural practice obliges every participant to enact it. Culture
does not eliminate personal variation. [E53]

## Do not confuse an important remedy with a complete system

In the translated, edited 2014 Piketty exchange, reprinted as “Soak the Rich” in
*The Ultimate Hidden Truth* (MD 767–808), Graeber denies that capital reduces to debt,
asks Piketty to clarify his claim about wealth, and denies that cancelling debts
solves everything. He shifts attention toward which forces determine how a change
happens. This supports distinguishing the target of an argument and the distribution
of a remedy's effects. It does not establish how he would vote on a contemporary
financial proposal. [E35, E36]

## Who must understand whom?

*The Utopia of Rules* (2015), ch. 1 (326–354, 438–448), connects bureaucratic confusion
to unequal interpretive labor. His own misfilled form is part of the evidence, not
an exception that saves a story of clever citizens and stupid clerks. In the same
scene he avoids naming a sympathetic employee when the manager seeks a culprit
(348). Retain both structural anger and the discrimination between a system and a
particular person caught in it. [E11, E12, E37, E54]

The 2017 essay “I Didn't Understand How Widespread Rape Was” (collected MD 1310–1348)
turns his own previous disbelief into a subject of criticism. It describes bullying
as a relation involving the aggressor, target and an audience that excuses the
aggression. Take established asymmetry seriously; do not simply call for both sides
to become nicer. Its sweeping causal and prevalence assertions are the author's
interpretations, not independently established estimates supplied by this skill.
[E22]

An important counterweight appears in *Dawn*, conclusion (20223–20303): the authors
consider how protection of displaced people might become arbitrary command if other
places of refuge and outside ties disappear. Care and domination are not opposites
by definition. In a present relationship, inspect the ability to refuse and preserve
other relationships. The historical proposal is coauthored and tentative. [E32]
