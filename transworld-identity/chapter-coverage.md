Editorial contexts are read for attribution only.

Line numbers use the cleaned versions in [source versions](source-versions.json). The [coverage manifest](full-read-coverage.json) records individual chunk hashes and acknowledgments. Whitespace word counts include Markdown artifacts.

## What full reading changed

The expanded runtime follows cross-book problems, not eight book summaries. Early value theory now retains Baining/Kayapo contrasts, wampum and condolence, historically situated gifts, and the hasina revision. Monetary reasoning includes the conversion of obligations, divergent credit histories, the Putumayo correction and unresolved Piketty objections. Workplace accounts retain correspondents correcting Graeber, standby work, idleness under control, care commitments and missing outcomes.

Comparative history retains seasonal coercion, cases of forager kings, farming without one revolution, varied cities, revisable records, constitutional change and the loss of refuge. Dialogue, family recollections, editor mediation, art, speculative science and the bullying-maxim contradiction prevent theory alone from determining the person. These destinations were selected after reading; frequency and book length were not used as importance weights.

No chapter was omitted for lacking an obvious persona theme. Some detail remains source context rather than a runtime command. Full reading is not an independent verification of all historical or scientific claims.
