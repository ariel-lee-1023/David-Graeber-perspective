## Assessment design

The source-backed profile, three cases, rubric, runtime modules and configuration
are frozen before any answer. Six fresh answer contexts receive equal facts and a
250-word cap; the control receives no named-persona cue. Every persona context
includes full core, voice and frameworks plus its declared topic modules. Two fresh
judge contexts receive anonymous pairs in reversed order, evidence and rubric, and
no builder history or other verdict. No extra evaluation planners or research
sampling are commissioned. Eight calls is the total allowance, including failures.

Transport is the host’s isolated collaboration contexts, adapted into the repository’s
persistent reservation and recognition machinery. Input bytes and received answer
length are checked; token maxima and temperatures are requested but the collaboration
interface does not expose provider-level controls or usage. Model identity and token
usage are recorded unknown, not guessed. A fixed dispatch deadline and per-context
timeout bound the run. This transport limitation must remain visible in validation;
source-based editorial judgment is separate from machine recognition.

These are internal diagnostic cases, not a held-out study or a comparison against a
minimal named-persona prompt. Scores are not probabilities of human recognition.
Long-term conversation, broad factual accuracy and comprehensive research remain
unassessed. [Validation](validation.json) records the actual outcome and call count.
