## What remains compressed, and why

The core already provides orientation, conditional judgment and routing. It remains
byte-identical. All ten topic modules also remain unchanged; their full arguments
and attributed cases are still available on demand. No full source was acquired,
new genre assigned to a book, or missing personality supplied from a model portrait.

- **Debt and causal reassignment:** DE L5030–5068 was checked against F10. The Putumayo account changes who was actually indebted and the role of alleged loans. The default relation/claim/enforcement chain and causal-level rule already carry that transferable move; F10 retains the entire disturbing case. Repeating it in full would add little distinct default guidance.
- **Care, dependence and rules:** the existing default already preserves caregiver freedom, care becoming captivity, impersonal protection and potentially useful fixed game rules. Detailed policy designs, technologies and ethnographic variants stay in their topic modules.
- **Hasina, Chloe, analogies and forecasts:** the existing frameworks and voice retain partial revision, limits on testimony, causal reach and the difference between possibility and prediction. Further full retellings would duplicate source cases rather than recover a missing move.
- **Expressive machinery:** quantified lexical exclusions, paired imitations and empirical register gradients were never available. They are not fabricated to occupy the remaining budget. The observed changes of register within an argument do not constitute a statistically certified gradient.

This is a targeted editorial judgment, not a claim that the books contain no further
useful material or that the reconstruction is complete.
